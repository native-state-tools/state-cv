State CV is a free CV builder that runs in your browser. Your CV stays on your device, and the PDF it makes is easy for job application systems (ATS) to read.

Open State CV

CV or resume?

A CV is your full career record: everything you have done. A resume is a shorter version picked for one job. State CV helps you keep the full record and export a clean PDF.

Three promises

No account, and nothing is uploaded.

Free, no account

No sign-up, no ads, and no tracking.

ATS-friendly

A plain layout and real text that application systems read correctly.

How it works

  1. Start a new CV or import a PDF you already have.
  2. Edit it. It saves in this browser as you type.
  3. Export a PDF or plain text, and download a backup.

FAQ

Where is my data?

Only in this browser on this device. Clearing the site's data, or switching browsers or devices, means it is not there. Read more in About and privacy.

How do I back up?

Open More and choose Backup (JSON). You can also open Your data and choose Download backup. Keep the file somewhere safe. To restore it, choose Import and pick that file.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything State CV does today is free with no account.

Does it work offline?

After the first visit, yes, in supported browsers.
